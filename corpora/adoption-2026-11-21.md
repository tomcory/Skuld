## Left out after the reviews

- **Firebase Analytics** (`firebase-analytics-data-collection`): no app's capture contains any Firebase Analytics
  traffic, so the passage would document nothing observed. Its tag, `google`, would show it for YouTube, Sign-in,
  Drive and the rest. Author's decision.
- **Play Integrity** (`android-play-integrity`): the tag on device characteristics broke the corpus's own rule. The
  page names no device characteristic, and 960 of the 1,011 device-characteristics claims are a device model name sent
  to fonts, YouTube or Microsoft hosts, not fraud checks. Shown in 273 of 274 stage-1 batches, it would have pushed
  purpose inference towards fraud prevention. Device characteristics stay undocumented; that is an honest gap.
- **Consent and delay sentences** of the Facebook and Firebase pages: consent and ePrivacy are outside the frame
  (paper, Sect. 2). Under gate (a), "don't send until consent" would pass as a removal.
- **Earlier exclusions, unchanged:**
  - e-mail, name and IP address (no documented alternative);
  - the long tail of ad networks;
  - CJEU Meta (Art. 6(1)(f); see the decisions);
  - CJEU Digi (built into the rules);
  - WP260's annex row on the legal basis;
  - Google Play's User Data policy.

## Known limits (tagging, retrieval)

- **The entity `google` merges Google's services.** Firebase, YouTube, Sign-in, Drive, Maps, fonts, Google's ad hosts
  and Keep's own backend all resolve to it. So `firebase-crashlytics-opt-in` is shown for every Google practice: 48 in
  the study, of which 16 involve a Crashlytics or Firebase host. It also counts as documentation for each of them.
- **Some AdMob traffic goes to `google`, not DoubleClick.** AdMob/GPT requests to googlesyndication, googleadservices
  and adtrafficquality resolve to `google` (31 practices), so they do not see the AdMob passage.
- **Two code defects, fixed in Skuld 0.11.4** (the author's decision of 21 Nov):
  - a practice whose datum is the root `data` matched every node-tagged passage;
  - stage 1 pooled passages per batch without saying which practice each was retrieved for. Passages are now labelled.
- **The cap order is alphabetical.** Which passage `PER_COMPONENT` drops depends on file names.
- **`recipient_types` are inert today.** `about` does not read them, so the `appsflyer` passage reaches only AppsFlyer
  practices. My earlier claim that it documented alternatives for InMobi, Vungle and Mixpanel was wrong. Dropping its
  types remains right for consistency.

## Decisions for the author

1. **WP260's good-practice examples:** keep them (leaving them out selects against the source). Their effect on the
   share judged not specific is uncertain in direction, so report it as a change between runs.
2. **Licences:** a short quotation for analysis (§ 51 UrhG) for the pages that state no licence (AdMob Help, Meta, the
   existing Play Console and AppsFlyer passages). Android Developers is under Apache 2.0 (developer.android.com/license),
   Firebase under CC BY 4.0.
3. **The `appsflyer` passage's recipient types:** drop them, for consistency.
4. **Further norms the reviews proposed:**
   - a balanced excerpt of WP203 Sect. III.2.2 (pp. 23–26) on how to weigh the compatibility factors, which is the
     thinnest context today;
   - EDPB 4/2019 para. 71 ("shall be assessed according to the criteria in Article 6(4)");
   - Art. 5(1)(a), which the frame lists for specificity;
   - WP203's layered-notice paragraph (p. 16);
   - Recital 39 sentence 2;
   - EDPB para. 76's "Relevance" element;
   - CJEU C-252/21 paras. 108–109: "just as effectively", cited by the paper, under Art. 6(1)(f);
   - WP203 p. 46 on tracking and behavioural advertising. It pushes towards "not compatible"; if included, take its
     fn. 113 with it.
5. **Lawfulness in the specificity context:** cut "as well as the legal basis for the processing" from Art. 13(1)(c)
   with […], so that a purpose is not judged unspecific for want of a legal basis?
6. **Component passages in stage 1:** the SDK passages say what a vendor *may* do with data. That raises supported
   advertising purposes, which could correct the paper's "ads rationalised as app performance" failure or overshoot.
   Accept it and say so in Sect. 4.2, or keep component passages out of stage 1.
7. **Further tagging:**
   - tag `android-identifiers-analytics` with the Advertising ID, the paper's showcase alternative. This needs the
     node cap raised to 4;
   - make `android-identifiers-principles`' comment consistent about the App Set ID;
   - order passages by the hierarchy of sources (GDPR, EDPB, WP29) rather than file name.
